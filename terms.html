Terms and Conditions

Last updated 1 October 2026

This is a practice app for students on ENG7101. It is free to use, and it is written and run by your teacher, Abdul Wahid. Please read this before you log in.

1. Your account

Your teacher gives you a username and a password. The account is yours alone. Do not share it, and do not log in as anybody else. One account works on up to two devices — normally a phone and a computer. If you need that reset, ask your teacher.

2. What is recorded, and why

Your teacher can see how you are getting on. That is the point of logging in: the app reports your practice so your teacher can help the students who need it.

Each time you practise, the app records:

  • which topic you did, when, and your score
  • how long the topic was open
  • how long you spent in the app, by day
  • when you last logged in, and whether you were on a phone or a computer

It does not record what you write. Your paragraphs and your answers to open questions stay on your own device — only the fact that you completed the task is reported.

3. Where it is kept

Your results are kept in your teacher's own private Google spreadsheet, which only your teacher can open. They are not sold, published, or given to anyone else, and they are not used for advertising. Your progress is also stored in your own browser so the app works offline.

4. How it is used

Your teacher uses this to see who is practising, who is finding something difficult, and what to go over in class. Practice scores are for feedback. They are not your course grade and they are not reported to the awarding body.

5. Fair use

Please use the app honestly. It is practice, so a score you did not earn only misleads your teacher about what you need. Do not try to interfere with the app or with other students' accounts.

6. The material

All the readings, questions and recordings in this app were written and produced by Abdul Wahid and are for your personal study on this course. Please do not copy them or pass them on.

7. Your choices

You can ask your teacher at any time to show you what has been recorded about you, to correct it, or to delete your account and the record with it. If you would rather nothing were recorded, simply do not log in — but then your teacher cannot see your progress or give you feedback on it.

8. Keeping it available

This is a free practice app offered alongside the course. It may be taken down, changed, or reset between intakes. Please do not rely on it as the only place your work is kept.

9. Questions

Ask Abdul Wahid, in class or by email.
